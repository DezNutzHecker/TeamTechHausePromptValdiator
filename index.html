What the AI Jailbreak Prompt Validator checks

Use the validator as a private browser-based prompt structure audit: paste a draft, inspect refusal risk, repair the weakest part, and decide whether the next move belongs in the Workshop, Failure Forensics, or F.R.A.N.K.

Prompt Structure Audit

Score one-shot prompts and multi-turn sequences across structure, specificity, escalation, cover depth, persuasion, and wording risk before a live model run.
